# Vegrow

> Vegrow is a Bengaluru-based B2B fruits marketplace founded in 2020 by IIT alumni that connects Indian fruit farmers with demand outlets across retail channels, using technology to match produce such as apples, bananas, pomegranates, and grapes with buyers, handling grading, sorting, quality assurance, and delivery, and paying farmers digitally. Backed by GIC, Prosus Ventures, Matrix Partners India, Elevation Capital, Lightspeed, and Ankur Capital, it raised a $46M round led by GIC in December 2023.
